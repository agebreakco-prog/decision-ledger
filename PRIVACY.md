# Decision Ledger: privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Decision Ledger is a Forge app built for the Atlassian Codegeist 2026 hackathon.

- **What it stores:** decisions it finds in your Confluence pages and Jira comments: the decision text, its reason, the Atlassian account ID of the person who decided, the date and a link to the source. It also stores warning flags it has shown.
- **Where:** only in Forge storage (Forge KVS) inside Atlassian's platform, in your site's installation. The app sends no data to any external server and uses no analytics or tracking.
- **Who can see it:** users of your Atlassian site who can use the app. People and team names are read live from Atlassian (Teamwork Graph) and are not copied elsewhere.
- **Deletion:** uninstalling the app removes its stored data under Atlassian's Forge data-retention rules.
- **Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/agebreakco-prog/decision-ledger/issues
